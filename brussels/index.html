Brussels captured me in the rain: an umbrella, a bronze kid behind a fence, then Grand Place turning the volume up. The pentagon is a walk. Heysel is a metro. I ate standing up and let the cobbles argue with my ankles.

Intel

The useful layer — Heysel tickets, STIB for the atom, and a square that does not take reservations.

🏛️ Grand Place

Gold guildhalls, a square that does the work. Night is the better photograph. You do not need a ticket. You need to look up.

⛲ Manneken Pis

Smaller than the souvenir. An umbrella helps. Two minutes, a fence, a bronze boy who has been famous too long. Then walk away.

🌳 Mont des Arts

A garden, a view, the city stacked. The photograph is the stairs. The museums next door if you still have eyes.

🇪🇺 Mini-Europe

The continent in miniature because the weather said so. Replicas you have already stood under elsewhere. Oddly soothing. Heysel, not the pentagon.

🎨 Street art

Comic murals, a wall that is not Grand Place. Walk the lanes off the gold. Brussels is funnier than the EU posters.

🪨 Cobbles

The pentagon is a walk. Ankles, rain, a city that expects both. This is how you actually see it.

⚛️ Atomium

Tickets on the official site. The view is the point. I did Mini-Europe and told myself the atom could wait. It is next door.

🍟 Frites

Twice-fried, a paper cone, sauce that is not ketchup unless you want the argument. Standing. This is the lunch.

€4–7

🧇 Waffles

Liège in the hand, Brussels on a plate. Sugar pearls if it is the dense one. Tourist carts will overdo the cream. Eat it anyway or don’t — the city will not mind.

€5–10

🍫 Chocolate

A shop that still smells like cocoa, not a mall. One bag. They do not travel as well as you hope, and you will eat them on the train.

€6–15

🍺 Beer

A glass that matches the beer, if the bar is paying attention. Trappist, lambic, something sour. Sit. This is not a flight for a story.

🦪 Moules-frites

A pot, a pile of shells, frites because Belgium said so. Messy. Share it or don’t finish it.

€18–26

🥘 Carbonnade

Beef in beer, sweet-savory, a winter plate. Frites again. The city repeating itself on purpose.

€16–22

📅 When to book

Weekends fill Grand Place hotels. A few weeks is enough unless it is Christmas. Rain is not a season. Pack the umbrella anyway.

📍 Where

Near Grand Place — tourist, loud, you walk out into the gold. Saint-Gilles / Ixelles — better evenings, actual restaurants, a tram back.

💶 Spend less

The waffle street is not a neighborhood. One metro out and the room gets honest.

🌸 April–June

10–20°C, terraces, a city that still rains. The umbrella in the photograph is not a prop.

☀️ July–August

18–25°C. Crowds on the square, frites weather. Heysel is easier than the pentagon at noon.

🍂 September–November

8–16°C, fewer people, beer that tastes like the season. One of the two best windows.

❄️ December–March

2–8°C, markets, gray that means it. Chocolate and a table. Pack a coat.
