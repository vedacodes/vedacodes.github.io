A city that leans.

Barcelona captured me in the stone that refuses a right angle, then in the streets that still do. The Sagrada is loud. The Gothic Quarter is quieter than the posters. Walking Port Vell at dusk — that’s what stayed, not the slogan.

📅 When to book

Gaudí interiors: weeks ahead in spring and summer. Eixample hotels cost the grid. 6–8 weeks for April–June and September.

📍 Where

Eixample — Gaudí on the way home. Gothic / Born — alleys, noise, walkability. Gràcia — plazas, kinder nights. Barceloneta if you want the beach more than sleep.

💶 Spend less

Metro, not a taxi from the airport unless the hour is unkind. Menú del día at lunch. Skip the hotel that faces the Sagrada unless you like bells.

🌸 March–May

15–22°C, terraces, a city that is still itself. Peak Gaudí crowds. Still the easiest Barcelona to like.

☀️ June–August

Hot, beach, late light. Hide at noon. The Gothic Quarter holds the heat.

🍂 September–November

The other window. Warm sea, fewer packages. I would go back in October.

❄️ December–February

Mild winters, empty-ish parks, a Sagrada without the full crush. Pack a layer.

🚶 Walk

The Gothic Quarter and the waterfront are a pedestrian day. Eixample blocks are longer than they look.
