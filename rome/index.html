📅 When to book

3–4 months ahead for April–June and September–October. 6–8 weeks for winter. Easter and Christmas: book the second you know you’re going.

📍 Where

Historic center — walk everywhere, pay for it. Trastevere — evenings, trattorie, actual neighborhood. Near the Vatican — art days. Termini — cheaper, easy trains.

💶 Spend less

Skip the hotels glued to monuments. Boutique B&Bs in residential streets. Sunday–Thursday is kinder. Breakfast included is not a small line item.

🌸 April–May

18–23°C, flowers, Easter crowds. Peak prices. Still the easiest Rome to love.

☀️ June–August

25–35°C. Long days, outdoor tables, and heat that files a complaint. Go if you must; hide at noon.

🍂 September–October

20–25°C, crisp, still busy in September. One of the two best windows.

❄️ November–March

8–15°C, shorter days, fewer people, lower rates. Some hours shrink. Cozy, if you pack a coat.

🚶 Walk

The historic center is a pedestrian argument you will lose, happily. Shoes, water, done.
