The useful layer — which ruins to book, when the hurricane window opens, and how not to overpay a taxi.

🏛️ Tulum ruins + beach

Maya cliffs over Caribbean water. Ruins first, then the sand below. Heat is part of the ticket — go early.

💧 Ik-Kil cenote

A green well with vines and a ladder. Swim if the line isn’t a parade. Sacred water, tourist rope, still worth it.

🔺 Chichén-Itzá

El Castillo, the ball court, serpent heads, skull stones. One of the New Seven Wonders — and a full day from Cancún.

🏝️ Isla Mujeres

Ferry, golf cart, quieter water. The Hotel Zone’s soft escape hatch.

🤿 Underwater museum

MUSA — sculptures underwater that want to become reef. Snorkel or dive. Surreal on purpose.

🏖️ Zona Hotelera beach

Powder sand, resort spine, turquoise that sells the brochure. Fine for a swim day between ruins.

🔺 Chichén-Itzá

ADO or a rental. Tickets at the gate or online. Combine with Ik-Kil if you still have legs. Midday is a furnace.

📅 When to book

Dec–Apr is peak. Spring Break is a different city if you’re not partying. Book hotels months ahead for winter; shoulder months are kinder.

📍 Where

Zona Hotelera — beach, buses, resort ease. Downtown Cancún — food, locals, cheaper nights. Near Puerto Juárez — ferry mornings without a taxi drama.

💸 Spend less

You don’t need oceanfront every night. ADO and a downtown bed stretch the ruins days.

☀️ December–April

Dry, 24–28°C, the brochure weather. Crowds and prices peak. Avoid Spring Break unless the party is the point.

🌺 May & November

Shoulder value — still beachable, fewer lines, better hotel rates.

🌧️ June–October

Hurricane season. Afternoon storms, humidity, cheapest rooms. Watch forecasts; August–October are riskier.

🎉 Local calendar

Carnaval, Day of the Dead, Independence Day — culture without needing Spring Break foam.

🚌 ADO / R1–R2
