Intel

The useful layer — park tickets, Genie-style line-skipping, and why Metro alone will not save a week here.

🏰 Disneyland

Anaheim, not LA proper. A full day minimum. Childhood, engineered. Coco found me in the park whether I planned it or not.

⭐ Walk of Fame

Hollywood Boulevard, stars underfoot, tourist density maxed. Go once. Hunt your people. Leave before the street performers own you.

🪞 Museum of Illusions

Rooms that argue with your eyes. Photos required. Laughing is the correct research method.

🔭 Griffith Observatory

The city below, the sign on the ridge. Free grounds. Sunset is a pilgrimage. Parking is the boss fight.

🎬 Universal Studios

Hogwarts on a hill, studio tour, rides that sell the movies back. A full day if you want more than a photo.

🎡 Santa Monica Pier

Ferris wheel, Pacific air, the week exhaling. Best at golden hour. The carnival is the point, not a bug.

🏰 Disneyland tickets

Date-based tickets on the Disney site. Genie+ / Lightning Lane if you hate standing still. Park Hopper only if you will actually use California Adventure the same day. Buy before you fly.

📅 When to book

Summer and holidays fill Anaheim and Hollywood. Fall (the real "LA summer") is kinder on rates and fog. Book park-adjacent hotels with free cancellation when you can.

📍 Where

Hollywood / Los Feliz — Walk of Fame, Griffith access. Santa Monica / Westside — pier, beach evenings. Anaheim — only if Disneyland owns a night or two. Near Universal — if the studio is the center of gravity.

💵 Spend less

Do not sleep in three neighborhoods in one week unless you like packing. One base + a car beats three Airbnbs. Parking is a line item — ask before you book.

🌸 March–May

15–24°C, clear before June Gloom, wildflowers after wet winters. Strong hiking and beach window.

☀️ June–August

18–29°C coast, hotter inland. June Gloom is real — gray mornings, clearer afternoons. Parks crowded. Valleys bake.

🍂 September–November

17–28°C. Best overall. "LA summer" is Sept–Oct: warm, clear, less fog. Fewer tourists. My preferred week.

🌤️ December–February

10–20°C, possible rain, still mild. Ski in the mountains and beach the same day if the sky allows. Lowest prices.

🚗 Rental car

Almost required for a week that includes Anaheim, Griffith, Santa Monica, and Universal. Budget parking everywhere. Freeways are the transit system.

Nearly required

🚕 Rideshare

Uber/Lyft for nights out and Griffith when parking is hopeless. Adds up fast across a week — better as a supplement than a plan.

🚇 Metro

Useful on a few corridors (Hollywood, Santa Monica Expo Line). Not a full substitute for Disneyland + hills + studios. TAP card, limited hours mindset.

🚶 Walk

Neighborhoods yes. City-wide no. Hollywood Boulevard and the pier are walkable clusters. Everything else is a drive.
