Not a fairy tale.
A city that holds its hill.

Prague is orange trees against spires if you come in fall, grey stone the rest of the year. The bridge is a crush. The river is the quiet. I walked both and still owe St Vitus the inside.

Intel

A castle ticket, a clock that does not need one, and beer that is cheaper than caution.

🌉 Charles Bridge

Walk it at dawn. The rest of the day is a market with statues. Malá Strana on one side, Old Town on the other.

🏰 Prague Castle

A hill, a cathedral, a changing of the guard. Circuit tickets on the castle’s own site. St Vitus is the interior I still owe myself.

🕐 Astronomical Clock

Walk-up. The show is short. The tower is a separate climb if you like roofs.

🎨 Lennon Wall

Free, painted, across from the French embassy. Five minutes, or an hour if you like watching it change.

✡️ Old-New Synagogue

Josefov. Jewish Museum tickets cover the other sites; the synagogue has its own door. Go with time, not a checklist sprint.

🌳 Kampa

An island park under the bridge. Sit. The city is loud above you.

🏰 Prague Castle

Official hrad.cz circuits. Buy there. A street seller is not a circuit.

🥟 Dumplings

🍩 Trdelník

A tourist trap with sugar. Fine once if you want the smell. Not Czech home cooking. Do not build a day on it.

Vepřo knedlo zelo if you are hungry. A plate, not a snack.

☕ Coffee

Third-wave in Vinohrady or the Old Town if you need a pause that is not beer. Both are correct.

📅 When to book

Christmas markets and May: book the second you know. Otherwise 4–6 weeks. Castle hotels cost the view.

📍 Where

Old Town — the clock, the prices. Malá Strana — under the castle, cobbles, nights. Vinohrady / Žižkov — trams, actual restaurants, kinder rates.

💶 Spend less

Tram, not a taxi up the castle road. Lunch specials. A beer hall that is not on the square.

🌸 April–June

15–22°C, gardens, a city that photographs easily. Crowds at the clock. Still easy to like.

☀️ July–August

Warm, packed bridges, long light. Go early to the castle. Hide at noon.

🍂 September–November

The window I actually got. Orange trees, grey stone, fewer packages after October.

❄️ December–March

Cold, markets, short days. Pretty if you packed a coat. The clock still performs.

🚶 Walk

Old Town to the bridge to Malá Strana is a pedestrian day. The castle is a hill. Shoes.
