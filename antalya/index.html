Not a resort brochure.
A harbor with a Roman gate.

I came for the water. I stayed for Perge — a colonnade you sit in, ruins you walk, a stadium outside the walls. Then Kaleiçi: Hadrian’s Gate, timber houses, streets that fill with tables after dark. The marina is boats and cliff houses. The sea is rocks. Three days is a first draft of a city that is both an old town and a coast.

Intel

A ticket for Perge that is not a parking-lot tour, a gate you walk through for free, and a visa that is not Schengen.

🏛️ Perge columns

A colonnade that still stands. You sit on the step and the pillars keep the sun honest. This is the photograph people mean when they say Perge.

🧱 Perge ruins

Towers at the gate, a street you walk, carved stones at the entrance. The city is larger than the colonnade. Wear the heat like a ticket.

🏟️ Perge stadium

Outside the walls. A long stone oval. I leaned on the masonry. The seats are a different sentence than the columns.

⚓ Harbor / marina

The old marina under the cliff houses. Fishing boats, a yacht, a boat ride that leaves from the same water. Kaleiçi drops into it.

🌊 The rocks

Mediterranean against stone, not a hotel pool. A short walk from the old town if the path is open.

🚪 Hadrian’s Gate

Three arches. The Roman door into Kaleiçi. Free. The square in front is the waiting room.

🏘️ Kaleiçi streets

Timber houses, a flag over an alley, tables after dark. The old town is the rest of the day once Perge is done.

🎫 Perge

Official archaeological-site ticket. Not a man at the parking lot with a laminated sheet. The theater has been closed for restoration — check the booth.

✈️ Getting here

Antalya Airport (AYT), then a transfer into Kaleiçi or Lara. Perge is east, toward Aksu — a taxi or a tour, not a stroll from the gate.

🏘️ Kaleiçi

Walk to the gate, the marina, the night tables. A pension in the old town is the practical base. Stairs, stone, a courtyard if you are lucky.

🏖️ Lara / Konyaaltı

The resort strip. Beach, a shuttle, a different city. I would try it only if Kaleiçi already felt small.

💶 Spend less

Prices are in lira. A marina table can eat the day. Perge is the line item that is worth it. The alleys are cheap once you have a room.

🌸 April–June

The coast behaves. Perge is hot by noon anyway. One of the two windows.

☀️ July–August

Mediterranean heat. Walk Perge at opening. Hide at noon. The marina still works after dark.

🍂 September–October

The other window. Softer sea, fewer day-trippers if you are lucky.

❄️ November–March

Milder than Cappadocia, still a coat at night. Some boat desks close. The old town does not.

🚶 Walk Kaleiçi

The old town is the transit. Hadrian’s Gate to the marina is stairs and stone. Shoes that forgive both.

🚕 Taxi to Perge

East, toward Aksu. Agree the price or use a meter app. The site is not a city-center stroll.

🚌 Tram / bus

Antalya has a tram. Useful for the museum and the airport corridor. Kaleiçi is still walking.

⛵ Marina boat

A short ride from the old harbor. Agree the fare. The coastline is the point, not the microphone.

🚗 Rental car

Useful if you want Aspendos and Termessos without a group. Parking in Kaleiçi is a mood.

Turkey is not Schengen. Indian citizens typically need an e-Visa (or a sticker visa) to visit Antalya. Apply only on the official e-Visa site. ETIAS does not apply here.

Seven chapters. Perge’s pillars, the ruins, the stadium outside the walls, the harbor and a boat, the rocks, Hadrian’s Gate, and Kaleiçi streets that kept the timber.

Pillars that still do the work

Perge’s colonnade is a room without a roof. I sat on the step between the drums. The stone is pale, the sky is a hard blue, and the columns keep choosing to stand. This is the ancient city in one photograph — then you have to walk the rest.

02 / 07

Ruins, a gate, a walk

The towers at the entrance are the first honest scale. Carved stones, a wall, a street you walk like the Romans left the paving. I found shade on a stair between two round towers. Perge is not one colonnade. It is a city that emptied and kept the masonry.

The stadium outside the walls

The filename said oustide. The stadium is outside. A long oval of stone. I leaned on the wall. The seats are a different grammar than the pillars — this is where a crowd used to sit, and now the grass does.

Harbor, marina, a boat

The old marina under the cliff. Fishing boats named like people. A yacht that paid more. I took a boat that left from the same water. Kaleiçi is above it. The harbor is the city’s handshake with the sea.

Rocks, then the Mediterranean

Not the hotel pool. Stone in the water, a cliff with pines, a horizon that does not negotiate. I photographed it because the marina is boats and this is the sea without a dock.

06 / 07

Hadrian’s three arches

The gate is how Kaleiçi still says Roman. Three arches, marble that got cleaned, a square that collects everyone. I stood in front of it in a dress that was not marble. The old town starts on the other side.

Kaleiçi, timber and a flag

Ottoman houses that kept the wood. Streets that fill with tables after dark. A Turkish flag hung over an alley like a ceiling. This is the town you sleep in if you came for the harbor and stayed for the gate.
