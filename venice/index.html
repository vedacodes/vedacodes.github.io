Not a postcard.
A city that runs on water.

The first day I kept hunting for a curb. Then I stopped. Venice is islands that decided streets were optional. The monuments are loud. The alleys are the trip — and Burano is the day you remember you can still leave the labyrinth.

📅 When to book

San Marco prices are a tax on the view. Book 6–8 weeks out in spring and fall. Carnival is a different country.

📍 Where

San Marco — you pay to hear the orchestras. Dorsoduro — Accademia, quieter nights. Cannaregio — actual grocery stores, kinder rates. Sleep near a vaporetto stop you will use.

💶 Spend less

A small hotel on a side canal. Skip the room with a palace view you will photograph once. Breakfast included is not a small line.

🌸 April–June

The city wakes up. Crowds, yes. Light on water. One of the two windows that behave.

☀️ July–August

Heat, cruise days, a crush on the Rialto. Go early or go somewhere else at noon.

🍂 September–November

The other good window. Acqua alta starts to visit. Pack for wet stone.

❄️ December–March

Fog, acqua alta, fewer people. Carnival if you booked it. Cold feet, good alleys.

🚶 Walk

The historic city is walkable until it isn’t — a dead end, then a bridge. Shoes that forgive stone.
