Intel

The useful layer — a 4am jeep for Tiger Hill, tea-garden timing, Mall Road on foot, and when the monsoon makes the hills hard.

🌄 Tiger Hill sunrise

Wake before 4am. Golden light on Kangchenjunga is the postcard. Book the jeep the night before. Carry a jacket — the ridge is freezing even in spring.

🍃 Tea gardens

Happy Valley and the terraced slopes that built the town’s name. Walk the rows, taste a flush if the estate is open, buy from authorized dealers.

🛕 Peace Pagoda

White Japanese Buddhist stupa above town. Free entry, sunset silhouettes, a quieter hour than Chowrasta. Walk or short taxi from the center.

🏔️ Kangchenjunga views

Clear mornings after monsoon or in spring. The third-highest peak does not always show — when it does, stop walking.

🚂 Toy train

Darjeeling Himalayan Railway — UNESCO, joy rides when steam is running. Book ahead on IRCTC. Window seats on the mountain side if you can choose.

🧭 Mall Road

Chowrasta, shops, evening air without engines. The town’s living room. Walk it once in daylight and once after the lamps.

🚙 Tiger Hill jeep

Hotel desks and local stands book shared or private jeeps for sunrise. Peak season sells out the night before. Confirm pickup time twice.

📅 When to book

March–May and October–November need a head start. Festival weekends fill Mall Road hotels. Monsoon is quieter and wetter.

📍 Where

Near Mall Road / Chowrasta — walk everywhere that matters in town. Quiet lanes above the market — sleep without jeep horns. Stay close enough that a 3:30am Tiger Hill pickup is not a negotiation.

💵 Spend less

Homestays beat view-premium rooms if Mall Road is a ten-minute walk. Shared jeeps beat private taxis for most day trips. Tea is cheaper at estates than airport gift shops.

🌸 Mar–May

10–20°C — best overall. Clear mountain views, First Flush tea, rhododendrons. Book hotels early. My preferred window with autumn.

🌧️ Jun–Sep monsoon

15–20°C, hard season for views — fog, landslides, washed-out plans. Lush tea gardens if you accept the weather. Not the Tiger Hill gamble.

🍂 Oct–Nov

8–18°C — post-monsoon clarity, Second Flush tea, festival energy. Excellent photography weather. Strong second choice after spring.

❄️ Dec–Feb

2–10°C, cold mornings, occasional snow dusting. Cozy cafés, budget rates, fewer crowds. Pack layers like you mean it.

🚙 Shared jeeps

How the hills actually move — NJP/Siliguri to Darjeeling, and day trips when full. ₹200–300 per seat on the climb; private taxis cost more.
