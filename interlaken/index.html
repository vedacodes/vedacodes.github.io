Not a postcard.
A valley that bills in francs.

Interlaken sat me down between Thun and Brienz and then sent me up. Harder Kulm in the wind, Lauterbrunnen like a hallway of water, Schilthorn in a cabin that refuses to pretend it’s a metro. The town is small. The vertical is not. I paid in francs and walked until the mountains had made their point.

⛰️ Harder Kulm

The hometown mountain. Funicular, two lakes, three peaks arguing in the distance. Sunset if the wind lets you stand still.

💧 Lauterbrunnen

A valley that looks like someone folded the earth and left the water on. Staubbach falling like a held note. I already went. I’d go again.

🚡 Schilthorn

Cable cars stacked like a dare. Piz Gloria at the top, Bond still hanging around the restaurant, Alps that don’t care about the movie.

🏘️ The town

Höhematte in the middle, streets that end in mountains. Interlaken is a hinge, not a capital. Walk it before you leave it.

🌊 Aare

The river between the lakes. Turquoise that looks edited. Sit on the bank. Don’t try to make it a water park.

🍂 Fall, walking

The trees did the talking. Gold on the slopes, a church steeple, a path that didn’t need a ticket. This is why I came when I did.

📅 When to book

Summer and foliage weekends: months ahead. Shoulder weeks: a month is enough. Snow weeks if you want skis, not if you want trails.

📍 Ost vs West

Interlaken Ost — the trains to Lauterbrunnen, Grindelwald, Lucerne. You hear them. Interlaken West — a little quieter, Lake Thun side, still a walk or a bus. I cared more about the morning train than the pillow.

🏔️ Lauterbrunnen

Quieter. You wake up in the valley instead of commuting into it. Waterfalls at breakfast. Fewer late restaurants. If the town feels like a hinge, stay on the door that opens to the cliffs.

🌸 April–June

Snow still on the peaks, trails opening, water loud. Shoulder crowds. Pack a layer you will actually wear.

☀️ July–August

Hiking weather, full trains, prices that know it. Go early on the mountains. The town is a corridor.

🍂 September–November

This is when I went. Gold trees, colder mornings, fewer people after the summer rush. Autumn doing all the talking.

❄️ December–March

Snow, short days, some paths shut. A different trip. Don’t pack like it’s September and hope.

🚶 Walk

The town is a walk. The mountains are not. Save your legs for the valley floors and the viewpoints, not for pretending Schilthorn is a stroll.
