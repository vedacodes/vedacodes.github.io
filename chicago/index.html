Chicago stole the week with Midwestern ease and a skyline that does not apologize. I walked the Riverwalk until the towers had names, stood under Cloud Gate with everyone else, and still made room for a pizza that is really a pie. Four days is a first draft of a city built to be walked and ridden.

🪞 Millennium Park

Cloud Gate — the Bean — and Crown Fountain. Start downtown here. Summer brings free concerts; any season brings a plaza that photographs itself.

🎨 Art Institute

Impressionists upstairs, lions outside. Plan half a day if you care about paintings. Timed entry keeps the crush honest.

🎡 Navy Pier

A pier into Lake Michigan — shops, food, the Centennial Wheel. Sunset from the wheel is the postcard; the walk out is the point.

🚤 Architecture river tour

From the water the skyline finally makes sense. Guided boats leave near the Riverwalk. Book a late slot if you want blue hour on the glass.

🍕 Deep-dish

Not a slice — a pie. Lou Malnati’s or Giordano’s if you want the legend. Arrive hungry; leave slower.

🎷 Blues & jazz

South Side blues rooms and downtown jazz. The city’s musical legacy is still live. One set beats another museum hour.

Buy timed tickets online. Weekends sell. Members and Illinois residents have their own rules — read the site before you line up.

📅 When to book

Summer festivals and marathon weekends need a head start. Fall is kinder on rates. Winter is cheapest if you can take the wind.

📍 Where

Loop / River North — walk to the Bean and the river. Riverwalk-adjacent — evenings that look like the postcard. Near a Brown/Green/Red stop — cheaper rooms without stranding yourself.

💵 Spend less

Do not pay skyline-view premiums if the Riverwalk is two blocks away. The CTA is the view. A solid mid-rise near a station beats a lobby you will Instagram once.

🌸 Spring (Apr–May)

8–20°C, city waking up, patios opening, fewer crowds than summer. Mild walking weather. Cubs season starts. Strong value window.

☀️ Summer (Jun–Aug)

20–30°C, peak season — festivals, lake beaches, long light. Book early. Expect crowds and hotel rates that notice.

🍂 Fall (Sep–Oct)

10–22°C — best overall. Color, architecture tours that do not melt you, marathon energy in October. My preferred window.

❄️ Winter (Nov–Mar)

−10–5°C, wind that earns the nickname. Markets, museums, indoor dining. Harsh outside; budget-friendly inside.

💡 Pro tips

  • Book the interview early — wait times swing hard by city and season
  • A visa stamp is not entry; CBP decides at the airport
  • ESTA is for VWP nationals only — Indian passports do not use ESTA
  • Keep answers consistent with DS-160
  • Print or save appointments, DS-160, and lodging notes offline

🪪 ID at the airport

  • REAL ID–compliant driver’s license or state ID, or
  • Valid passport (Indian passport works as ID for TSA), or
  • Other TSA-accepted ID

Airlines and TSA set the bar for boarding — not a new visa stamp.

📄 Carry anyway

  • Passport
  • I-797 / EAD / I-20 as relevant to your status
  • Recent pay stubs or school letter if you like a paper trail
  • Return flight confirmation

💡 Tips

  • This is not international — no CBP entry stamp for Chicago
  • If your status is expiring soon, fix that before you treat this like a casual weekend
  • Leaving the US (e.g. to Nassau or Mexico) is a different problem — re-entry needs a valid visa/stamp
  • REAL ID enforcement for air travel: check TSA’s current list
Important: Status questions are individual. When in doubt, check USCIS / your immigration counsel — not a blog comment thread.
