📅 When to book

Summer and Christmas need a head start. Fall — when the trees do the work — is kinder. A few weeks is usually enough.

📍 Where

Mitte — the monuments, the walk, the prices. Kreuzberg / Neukölln — evenings that are not a gate. Prenzlauer Berg — quieter, still a U-Bahn.

💶 Spend less

Do not pay extra to sleep in the photo of the gate. The S-Bahn is the view. A courtyard building off the ring is the Berlin room.

🌸 March–May

8–18°C, parks opening, fewer lines. Easy walking weather. The city without the summer crush.

☀️ June–August

18–26°C, long light, lakes, festivals. Book the dome. Hide at noon if you melt.

🍂 September–November

8–16°C, the trees I actually photographed. Festival of Lights if you hit October. One of the two best windows.

❄️ December–February

−2–5°C, markets, short days, museums that earn their heat. New Year at the gate is a crowd, not a plan unless you like that.

🚶 Walk

Mitte is walkable. The rest of Berlin pretends it is. Shoes. Then the U-Bahn when the next pin is a neighborhood away.
